# Kitsch - Viral Content Clipping
## Campaign Requirements

**Agency:** Clipping Culture  
**Budget:** $21,875 | **CPM:** $2/1K views  
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels

---

### What To Do

Download clips from the official Kitsch content folder and post them to your clip pages on TikTok and Instagram Reels.

**Content folder:**  
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/10LDShzCx8aXB4lucsJIqQ2zTv7EAmVy5?usp=drive_link

⚠️ Do NOT use footage from any source outside the official content folder.

Content should feel native to social media — not like a branded ad. Focus on:
- Strong hooks and surprising transformations
- Educational haircare content (hair growth, thinning hair, hair loss, breakage, frizz, heatless curls, hair perfume, shampoo bars, healthy hair routines)
- Problem / solution formats and viral storytelling angles
- Re-cutting existing winning footage with new hooks or text overlays
- Trend-based edits when relevant
- Clips that maximize watch time, shares, comments, and saves

---

### Tagging Requirement (REQUIRED)

You must tag the official Kitsch accounts in the caption of every post:
- **TikTok:** @kitsch
- **Instagram:** @mykitsch

---

### Post Specs

- ✔ Download and post ONLY content from the official Kitsch content folder
- ✔ Caption must tag @kitsch (TikTok) or @mykitsch (Instagram)
- ✔ High-quality posts only — no blurry or low-effort content
- ✔ Video must be AT LEAST 10 seconds long
- ✔ English content only
- ✔ Post must stay live for 30 days minimum

### Performance Requirements

- ✔ Minimum 0.20% engagement rate (1,000 views = minimum 2 likes)
- ✔ Likes must be visible — hidden likes = automatic rejection
- ✔ Page audience should primarily be Tier 1–2 countries

---

### Not Allowed

- ❌ Content from any source not in the official Kitsch folder
- ❌ Captions missing the required tag
- ❌ Low-quality or lazy uploads
- ❌ NSFW, violent, or controversial content
- ❌ Bait posting, spam posting, story boosting, paid boosting
- ❌ Removing the post before 30 days
